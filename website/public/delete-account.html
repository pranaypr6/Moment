Data Deletion Request

If you wish to delete your Moment account and all associated data (including your photos, connections, and personal information), you have two options:

Option 1: In-App Deletion (Recommended)

The fastest way to delete your data is directly within the Moment app:

  1. Open the Moment app.
  2. Tap on the **Settings** icon.
  3. Tap **Delete Account**.
  4. Confirm your decision. Your account and all data will be permanently deleted immediately.

Option 2: Email Request

If you no longer have access to the app, you can request account deletion by emailing our support team:

Send an email to: pranayburra66@gmail.com

Please send the email from the Google account associated with your Moment profile and include "Account Deletion Request" in the subject line. We will process your request within 14 days.
