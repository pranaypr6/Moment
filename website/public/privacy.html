Privacy Policy for Moment

Last updated: July 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you use Moment, we collect:

  • Account Information: Email address, Google profile information, and a chosen username.
  • User-Generated Content: Photos (moments) and notes you choose to share with your connected partner.
  • Device Information: Device IDs (like FCM tokens) required to deliver push notifications and update your wallpaper.
  • Presence and Interaction Signals: Lightweight signals (such as "thinking of you" pings or your current status/vibe) that you choose to send, which are shared only with your connected partner.
  • Diagnostic and Usage Data: We use Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Analytics to automatically collect crash reports, device model, OS version, and app usage data (such as which screens are used and how often), so we can find and fix bugs and understand how the app is used. This data is tied to an anonymous installation identifier, not your name or email.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to provide the Moment service: delivering photos to your partner, sending you notifications when you receive a moment, updating your device's wallpaper, and sharing presence/interaction signals with your partner. We also use diagnostic and usage data to identify crashes, fix bugs, and improve the app's reliability and features.

3. Data Sharing

Your moments, notes, and presence/interaction signals are shared ONLY with the single partner you have explicitly paired with via an invite code. We do not sell your personal data or photos to any third parties.

We use a small number of service providers to operate the app, who process data on our behalf under their own data protection terms: Google Firebase (authentication, push notifications, crash reporting, and analytics) and Cloudflare (secure storage for the photos you send). These providers do not use your data for their own purposes.

4. Data Deletion

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from within the app (Settings > Delete Account) or by visiting our Data Deletion Request page.
