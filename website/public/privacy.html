Privacy Policy for Moment

Last updated: July 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you use Moment, we collect:

  • Account Information: Email address, Google profile information, and a chosen username.
  • User-Generated Content: Photos (moments) and notes you choose to share with your connected partner.
  • Device Information: Device IDs (like FCM tokens) required to deliver push notifications and update your wallpaper.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information exclusively to provide the Moment service: delivering photos to your partner, sending you notifications when you receive a moment, and updating your device's wallpaper.

3. Data Sharing

Your moments are shared ONLY with the single partner you have explicitly paired with via an invite code. We do not sell your personal data or photos to any third parties.

4. Data Deletion

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from within the app (Settings > Delete Account) or by visiting our Data Deletion Request page.
